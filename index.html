The problem

Friend trips die in the group chat. Everyone says yes, but nobody commits to a place, dates or money, so the first work excuse ends the plan.

Who it's for

The organiser: the friend who starts the plan and ends up chasing everyone.

What this shows

One trip, from "we should go" to a locked place, dates and a pooled deposit.

Prototype for the Scapia product assignment. Names, budgets and payments are made up.
